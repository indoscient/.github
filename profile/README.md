## 🛡️ Indoscient – Privacy Engineering for a Compliant Tomorrow

> **"Share the Value, Not the Data."**

Indoscient is a privacy engineering firm specialising in **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDPA) compliance** for Indian startups and enterprises. Unlike traditional legal consultancies, we implement privacy controls directly into your codebase — delivering functional solutions, not just policy documents.

---

### 🌍 Our Mission

Empower organisations to collaborate on data-driven insights while guaranteeing absolute privacy, regulatory compliance, and zero exposure of raw information. We envision a global ecosystem where every valuable dataset can be safely shared, analyzed, and monetized—fueling breakthroughs in health, finance, security, and beyond.

### 🔒 Core Technologies

- **End-to-End Encryption** – Data encrypted at rest & in transit; never leaves owner's vault
- **Federated Learning Engine** – Train ML models on encrypted shards; exchange only model weights
- **Differential-Privacy Guarantees** – Mathematically proven anonymity with privacy budgets
- **Smart-Contract Escrow** – Automatic payments upon verifiable model-performance thresholds
- **Blockchain Audit Trails** – Immutable logs for regulators and auditors
