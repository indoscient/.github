## 🛡️ INDOSCIENT – Privacy Engineering & DPDP Act Compliance

**Tagline:** Share the Value, Not the Data

> Privacy-first Data Exchange Platform enabling organizations to collaborate on data insights with **minimal data exposure architecture**. Uses end-to-end encryption, federated learning, and blockchain escrow for compliant data monetization under DPDP Act frameworks.

INDOSCIENT is a privacy engineering firm specialising in **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP Act) compliance** for Indian startups and enterprises. Unlike traditional legal consultancies, we implement privacy controls directly into your codebase — delivering functional solutions, not just policy documents.

---

### 🌍 Our Mission

To implement privacy-by-design engineering that enables organizations to meet DPDP Act, GDPR, and global compliance requirements without sacrificing product velocity. We build functional solutions — consent flows, encryption architectures, audit trails — directly into your codebase.

---

### Vision

A global ecosystem where data-driven collaboration happens securely — organizations share insights while keeping raw datasets in owner-controlled environments through minimal data exposure architecture, federated learning, and blockchain-backed trust.

### 🔒 Core Technologies

- **End-to-End Encryption** – Data encrypted at rest & in transit; minimal data exposure architecture
- **Federated Learning Engine** – Train ML models on encrypted shards; exchange only model weights
- **Differential Privacy Guarantees** – Differential privacy guarantees with defined ε budgets
- **Smart-Contract Escrow** – Automatic payments upon verifiable model-performance thresholds
- **Blockchain Audit Trails** – Immutable logs for regulators and auditors
